What these words mean

Reference

Open glossary
Organic Google visits
People who found the site in regular Google results and clicked. These are not paid ads.
Search visibility
How many times the site appeared in Google results. It is exposure, even if no one clicked.
Google ranking average
The average spot where the site appeared in search. Lower is better: 1 is the top result.
Engaged visit
A visit where someone stayed, viewed more than one page, or triggered a tracked action.
Valuable action
A tracked action that matters to the business, such as a form, booking, phone tap, or signup.
